Official RRDBNet Architecture

Uses the official 23-block Residual-in-Residual Dense Network trained on high-order realistic degradations to synthesize authentic photographic textures rather than blurry interpolation.

⚡

Cross-Platform Acceleration

Accelerated via optimized PyTorch tensor inference with streaming tiled execution, processing multi-megapixel images rapidly on server hardware without client memory thrashing.

🔒

Private & Temporary Storage

Photos are processed securely on our server. Images are assigned random UUID filenames, kept in temporary storage, automatically deleted after 1 hour, and never shared or retained.

ℹ️

Important Note on Neural Super-Resolution

Real-ESRGAN dramatically improves perceived sharpness, eliminates compression artifacts, and reconstructs fine natural textures using deep learning. However, it cannot guarantee recovery of true microscopic optical information that was never recorded by the physical camera sensor.

ABOUT CLEARPIX

Designed for photographers & creators.

True Neural Reconstruction

Traditional image upscaling tools rely on bicubic or Lanczos resampling, which only mathematically blur existing pixels. ClearPix runs deep convolutional neural networks that hallucinate realistic micro-details, restore edges, and clean compression noise.

Privacy-First Architecture

ClearPix processes images on our secure self-hosted server. Images are never stored in public directories, shared with third-party cloud APIs, or used for model training. Once processed, temporary files are automatically purged after 1 hour.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

What is Real-ESRGAN x4plus?

Real-ESRGAN is an advanced blind super-resolution model developed by ARC Lab at Tencent PCG and Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology. It models complex combinations of blur, noise, downsampling, and JPEG compression to reconstruct clean, high-resolution photographs.

Can I use ClearPix on mobile and desktop devices?

Yes. ClearPix is fully responsive and works on Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, macOS, and Linux across all modern browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). AI enhancement is executed on the server, so you get high-quality super-resolution on any device regardless of your client hardware.

How does server-side AI processing work?

When you upload a photo, it is received securely by our backend server and passed through the pretrained Real-ESRGAN x4plus neural network. The server uses hardware acceleration (such as GPU tensor acceleration or high-throughput CPU inference) to synthesize fine details, returning a crisp, high-resolution PNG to your browser.

How are large images handled?

Large panoramic and high-megapixel photos are divided into 512×512 tiles with overlapping padding. Completed tiles stream directly to CPU memory, preventing unified VRAM thrashing and eliminating seam artifacts.

Are my uploaded photos private?

Yes. Images uploaded to ClearPix are stored temporarily on the server solely to generate your enhanced preview and download. Files are saved with random UUID filenames in a private temporary directory and automatically deleted after 1 hour. Images are never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used to train AI models. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
